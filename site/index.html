Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag Literature Screener into Applications.
  2. Open the app. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify it (test versions are not yet signed with Apple). Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. “Chip: Apple M…” = Apple chip; “Processor: Intel” = Intel.

  1. Double-click the downloaded installer. It installs for your account only and needs no administrator rights; the app opens when it is done.
  2. If “Windows protected your PC” appears, click More info → Run anyway (test versions are not yet signed).

Installing not allowed on your computer? Try the portable version: extract the zip and double-click Literature Screener.exe. If your university blocks it, please tell us rather than working around your IT rules.
